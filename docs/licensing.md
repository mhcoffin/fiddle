# Licensing

This document records the licensing basis currently used for Fiddle. It is a
project record, not legal advice or a substitute for the applicable licence
agreements.

Fiddle is owned and developed by an individual. No company or other legal
entity is involved in its ownership or development.

Copyright in the Fiddle source code is reserved by its owner. All rights are
reserved. Publication of the source code does not, by itself, grant permission
to copy, modify, redistribute, or sublicense it.

## JUCE

Fiddle uses JUCE 9 under the free JUCE Starter commercial licence and the
[JUCE 9 End User Licence Agreement](https://juce.com/legal/juce-9-licence/).
Fiddle does not rely on JUCE's alternative AGPLv3 licensing option.

As of September 2, 2026:

- Fiddle has one Framework User: its individual owner and developer.
- No company or other legal entity owns or develops Fiddle.
- Fiddle generates no revenue or funding from its use of JUCE.
- The applicable JUCE-related revenue and funding total is therefore below the
  Starter licence limit of USD 20,000 over the preceding 12 months.

The owner will keep a record of any additional Framework Users who contribute
to or modify code or other copyrightable work that directly or transitively
depends on JUCE.

## Review Triggers

This licensing decision must be reviewed before any of the following occurs:

- JUCE-related revenue or funding approaches or exceeds the Starter limit.
- Another developer begins contributing to JUCE-dependent parts of Fiddle.
- Ownership of Fiddle moves to a company or other legal entity.
- Fiddle begins generating donations, sponsorship, advertising revenue, or
  other direct or indirect JUCE-related income.
- Fiddle changes to a new major version of JUCE with different licence terms.
- Fiddle changes from an all-rights-reserved project to an open-source licence.

Before an official binary release, the project should also include an
appropriate copyright notice and third-party notices for JUCE and other
distributed dependencies.
