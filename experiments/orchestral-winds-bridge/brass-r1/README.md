Display name: **Fiddle Bridge - Orchestral Brass r1 - INSPECTION ONLY**.
36 add-on dimensions plus one base; 37/52 conduits, 18 CCs and 19 keyswitch
pitches (KS0–18), 15 spare. There are 25 base rows, 71 add-on rows, 75 distinct
encoded PT IDs and 14 explicit mutex groups.

## Instruments and vocabulary

| Instrument family covered | Context-specific additions |
| --- | --- |
| French horn, Wagner tuba | Stopped/open horn, cuivré, applicable mutes and lip effects |
| Piccolo/B-flat/C/D/E-flat trumpets, cornet, flugelhorn, bass trumpet | Mute/stem choices, plunger, tonguing, pitch/entry/exit effects |
| Alto/tenor/bass/contrabass trombones | Slide, smear, glissando/portamento, mutes, growl and lip effects |
| Euphonium/baritone horn, bass/contrabass tubas | Multiple/flutter tonguing, applicable mutes, alternate fingering, multiphonic and pitch intent |

Shared general articulation/dynamics and wind dimensions match the woodwind
profile where present. Stopped horn, cuivré, shake, slide, smear and rip extend
the brass vocabulary. The section profile is a union, not a claim that every
instrument/library can realize every listed effect or compound.

## Mutes and cancellation

The main mute enum has Muted, Open 1, Half-muted, Bell mute, Bucket mute, Cup mute,
Straight mute and all three canonical Harmon-stem variants. Open is an explicit
value; unspecified is not an instruction to play open. Generic Muted is not
silently equated to a particular physical mute.

Plunger is a separate flag, allowing plunger with an inserted mute. Stopped horn
is also separate; it is not automatically equated to Muted or transposed by the
bridge. Explicit Open participates in separate Open/Stopped and Open/Plunger
mutex groups so it cancels those states. Cuivré remains independent of mutes,
stopping, accent and dynamic level. Natural is not a blanket mute/effect reset.
All these new grouping and cancellation choices are proposals requiring host
acceptance; there is no claim that Dorico already composes every combination.

Flutter tongue is separate from the Double/Triple tongue enum. Growl and
multiphonic intent are independent, as are entry and exit effects. No intermediate
pitches, extra notes, stopped-horn pitch correction or continuous pitch curves
are generated by this map. Existing Dorico performance data must not be duplicated.

Flute/reed key clicks, whistle tones, jet whistles, slap tongue, explicit flute
harmonics, generic vocal production, guitar-only mechanisms and percussion
controls are outside this default brass profile. See [inventory.md](inventory.md)
for every built-in ID's disposition. Further extensions can be customized later.

## Review and status

Import into Library > Expression Maps in a disposable Dorico project for
inspection alongside the other candidates. Review the original conditional
bases, main mute enum, independent Plunger/Stopped/Cuivré actions and the two
explicit Open cancellation groups. Import alone does not assign an endpoint.
**Not host-tested or runtime-compatible:** do not assign this to production Fiddle
or a VSTi; no matching decoder exists. No plugin or playback-template changes
have been made.

One voice per routed endpoint/port/channel. The candidate musical range remains
MIDI21–114; extreme tuba/contrabass-trombone pedal pitches may fall below it and
are outside this wire-range contract. Verify sounding/transposed ranges before
production assignment. The future decoder must consume command notes/releases
before downstream routing. Mid-passage initialization, replacement, expiry,
cancellation and the new mutex groups remain host acceptance work within the
supported voice/range scope.
